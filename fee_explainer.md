\*\*Fee Explainer: Brokerage and other charges on trades\*\*  
\- Brokerage is Groww's per-order fee: ₹20 or 0.1% of the order value, whichever is lower, with a ₹5 minimum.  
\- Statutory and exchange charges apply on top of brokerage: STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover fees and stamp duty. These are set by the government, regulator or exchange, not by Groww.  
\- 18% GST applies to brokerage and to several other charges, including exchange transaction, SEBI turnover and DP charges.  
\- Selling delivery shares incurs a DP charge (₹16.5 per sell transaction, ₹0 if the debit value is under ₹100) plus a depository fee and GST. Intraday DP charge is ₹0.  
\- Event-based charges also exist, such as ₹50 per position when open intraday positions are auto squared-off, and delayed payment charges of 0.05% per day.  
\- Because several charges apply per order and per side, the total deducted on a trade can exceed the ₹20 brokerage alone. Groww's help page explains each charge shown in the report.
